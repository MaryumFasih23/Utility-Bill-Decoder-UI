WTQ  Build  Track  Participant  Guide  Utility  Bill  Decoder  
1.  The  challenge  
Most  people  find  their  electricity  bill  hard  to  follow.  It  mixes  tariff  slabs,  taxes,  fuel  adjustments  and  many  
line
 
items,
 
and
 
each
 
provider
 
lays
 
it
 
out
 
differently.
 
Your
 
challenge
 
is
 
to
 
build
 
an
 
AI-powered
 
assistant
 
that
 
reads
 
a
 
bill,
 
pulls
 
out
 
its
 
details,
 
and

in
 
plain

.
 
The  input  is  an  electricity  bill  from  K-Electric,  LESCO  or  IESCO,  as  a  scanned  image,  screenshot  or  photo.  
Layouts

bills

This  guide  explains  the  data,  output  formats,  submission  requirements  and  scoring.  
1.1  The  two  levels  
The  challenge  has  two  levels.  Both  levels  are  required.  
Level  Task  Required  output  
Level  1:  Extraction  
Extract  non-identifying  billing  details,  readings,  charges,  taxes,  and  payable  amounts.  
One  JSON  object  per  bill  
Level  2:  Explanation  and  questions  
Answer  customer  questions  using  the  bill.  Some  answers  require  calculations  or  estimates.  
One  text  answer  per  bill  and  question

5.2  General  extraction  rules  
These  rules  apply  to  every  field  in  the  JSON.  ●  Extract  values  as  printed.  Do  not  recalculate,  round,  or  correct  bill  amounts.  ●  Amounts  are  numbers  in  PKR.  Remove  currency  labels  and  commas:  Rs.  19,964 becomes  19964.  ●  Credits  and  subsidies  are  negative.  For  example,  38209CR becomes  -38209.  Record  a  subsidy  as  a  
deduction.

Field  What  to  return  
provider  One  of  KE ,  LESCO ,  or  IESCO .  
tariff  The  tariff  code  exactly  as  printed,  such  as  A1-R  or  A-1a(01) .  
sanctioned_load_kw  Sanctioned  load  in  kW,  labelled  Sanc  Load,  S.Load,  or  San  Load.  
bill_month  The  billing  month  in  YYYY-MM  format.  
reading_date ,  issue_date ,  due_date

units_consumed  The  units  billed  for  the  current  month,  as  printed.  
charges

taxes

payable_within_due_date  The  amount  payable  by  the  due  date.  
payable_after_due_date  
The  amount  payable  after  the  due  date.  If  several  late-payment  amounts  are  shown,  use  the  highest.

5.4  Charge  and  tax  types  
Choose  a  type from  the  tables  below  for  each  entry.  The  printed  section  determines  whether  a  line  
belongs

5.4.1  Charge  types  Use  one  of  these  types  for  each  entry  in  charges.  
Type  Use  for  
energy  
Cost  of  units  used,  such  as  Variable  Upto  ...  Units,  Cost  of  Electricity,  or  Energy  Charges.  
fixed  Fixed  charges.  
fpa  Fuel  price  adjustment.  Use  fpa  for  both  FPA  and  FCA.  
quarterly_adjustment  Quarterly  tariff  adjustment,  such  as  QTA  or  Uniform  Quarterly  Adjustment.  
surcharge  Surcharges,  such  as  Additional  Surcharge  (PHL)  or  F.C  Surcharge.  
meter_rent  Meter  rent  or  service  rent.  
subsidy  Government  subsidy  or  relief  lines.  Record  the  amount  as  negative.  
other  Any  other  charge  line.   
5.4.2  Tax  types  Use  one  of  these  types  for  each  entry  in  taxes.  
Type  Use  for  
gst  Sales  tax  or  GST,  including  GST  on  FPA.  
electricity_duty  Electricity  duty,  often  labelled  ED.  
income_tax  Income  tax.  
municipal_tax  Municipal  or  local  charges  printed  under  taxes,  such  as  KE  MUCT  (KMC).  
other_tax  Any  other  tax  line,  such  as  extra  tax,  further  tax,  or  TV  fee.
